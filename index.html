To understand our rebranding needs, we analyzed Tanta Business Faculty's position against key competitors: the elite segment (AUC), the modern private segment (GUC/BUE), and traditional peers (Cairo/Alexandria). The data reveals a critical gap in "Perceived Innovation" despite high academic rigor.
